# Hybrid Renewable Energy System: Savonius VAWT & Automatic Solar Tracker

A proof-of-concept hybrid renewable power platform combining a drag-based Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (Savonius Rotor) with a closed-loop single-axis solar tracking panel.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This system explores multi-source micro-generation by pairing omnidirectional wind energy harvesting with active solar tracking:
* **Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT):** Utilises a Savonius rotor design that operates on differential drag forces between concave and convex blades, eliminating the need for wind-yaw alignment mechanisms.
* **Automatic Solar Tracker:** Tracks maximum solar irradiance using light sensors (LDRs) and feedback control to actuate panel inclination/orientation, optimising daily energy yield.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Specifications & Subsystems

### 1. Wind Turbine Subsystem (Savonius Rotor)
* **Turbine Type:** Drag-based Vertical Axis Wind Turbine (VAWT).
* **Working Principle:** Rotation generated by the differential drag coefficient ($\Delta C_d$) between the concave ($C_{d, \text{concave}} \approx 2.3$) and convex ($C_{d, \text{convex}} \approx 1.2$) surfaces relative to incoming wind vectors.
* **Key Advantages:** Self-starting capability at low wind speeds, omnidirectional performance (yaw-independent), low maintenance structure.

### 2. Active Solar Tracking & Control
* **Sensor Array:** Differential LDR / photodiode sensor bridge measuring ambient light intensity across axes.
* **Actuation & Logic:** Closed-loop controller translating analog voltage deltas into motor steps to align the PV panel perpendicular to sunlight vectors.
